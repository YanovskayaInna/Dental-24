Crown in one visit

Crown or bridge on site in 60 minutes – no second appointment

Forget the two-week wait for the lab, unpleasant impression material and wobbly temporaries. We design, mill and place your permanent tooth right here, in a single visit.

  1. 013D scan in 3 minutes

    No trays of impression material and no gagging. A digital camera creates an exact 3D model of your teeth.

    3 min

  2. 02Digital design

    Our dentist designs the anatomy of your crown on screen, with micrometre precision and taking your bite into account.

    10 min

  3. 03Milling the ceramic in 15 minutes

    A Swiss-German milling unit with diamond burs mills the crown from a solid block of feldspar ceramic or zirconia.

    15 min

  4. 04Firing and fitting in the chair

    Custom glaze in the oven, polish matched to the colour of your neighbouring teeth and secure fitting. You go home with your new tooth!

    20 min
Total time approx. 60 minutes
Cost: CHF 180

Crown & bridge in 60 min

We make crowns and bridges right here at our practice: digitally planned and placed in a single visit.

  • No temporary crown and, as a rule, no second appointment
  • Digital 3D scan instead of unpleasant impression material
  • Tooth-coloured ceramic, matched to the shape and colour of your teeth
  • Whether a crown or bridge is suitable for you is clarified beforehand in the examination
Procedure

3D scan, digital design, milling of the ceramic, firing and fitting: all right here at our practice.

Straumann implants

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth and serves as a stable base for a crown, bridge or denture. We work with Swiss premium systems from Straumann.

  • Careful planning before the procedure
  • Possible for one or several missing teeth
  • Designed to last, with good care and regular check-ups
  • Personal advice on procedure, duration and costs
Good to know

Whether an implant makes sense in your case depends on the findings. We discuss the options in a personal consultation.

Wurzelbehandlung

In root canal treatment we clean inflamed or infected root canals so that your own tooth can be kept.

  • Gentle treatment of the root canals
  • The aim is to relieve symptoms and preserve the tooth
  • Afterwards the tooth is permanently restored, for example with a filling or crown
  • Careful explanation of each step beforehand
Good to know

Whether a tooth can be saved is assessed after examination and X-ray.

Tartar removal & prevention

Professional teeth cleaning removes tartar and plaque, including in places that are hard to reach with a toothbrush.

  • Removal of tartar and soft plaque
  • Prevention of cavities and gum inflammation
  • Smooth tooth surfaces and fresh breath
  • Personal care tips for home
Good to know

Regular prevention supports the health of teeth and gums. How often it makes sense, we discuss individually with you.

Fillings & consultation

We advise you individually and treat cavities gently with fillings that suit your tooth.

  • Treatment of cavities and minor tooth defects
  • Fillings matched to tooth colour and bite
  • Clear explanation of the options
  • Transparent information on costs beforehand
Good to know

After the examination we recommend the right solution for your tooth and discuss it together with you.

Emergency & pain

If you have acute toothache, call us: we will quickly find an appointment.

  • Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 you can reach us directly by phone
  • Saturday and Sunday: emergency service for acute pain
  • Briefly describe your symptoms so that we can schedule you appropriately
Important

In case of severe facial swelling or difficulty breathing or swallowing, please contact the medical emergency number (144) immediately.
